## 📌 My projects

**1. [Olist E-Commerce Analysis — What Makes Customers Happy or Angry?](https://github.com/Viveksaini108/olist-ecommerce-analysis) (Real Data)**
Real data: 99,441 orders from Olist, a Brazilian online marketplace. I downloaded 8 tables with Python, joined them with SQL, and cleaned real problems like duplicate reviews and missing delivery dates. Biggest finding: late delivery decides the review — on-time orders average 4.29 stars, orders delayed 8+ days average just 1.73 stars. Only 3.1% of customers ever buy a second time.

**2. [Indian E-Commerce Sales & Returns Analysis](https://github.com/Viveksaini108/indian-ecommerce-sales-returns-analysis) (Sample Data)**
Sample data created by me, modelled on real Indian e-commerce patterns. I cleaned a messy file of 10,000 orders (mixed dates, duplicates, city names written many ways) and worked out the profit on every order. Findings: COD orders return far more than prepaid (18.3% vs 12.7%), Fashion returns the most (23.4% — top reason: size issue), and discounts above 40% lose money.

**3. [Indian Data Analyst Job Market Analytics](https://github.com/Viveksaini108/indian-data-analyst-job-market-analytics) (Sample Data)**
Sample data generated by me, modelled on real market patterns. I analysed 1,200 data analyst job postings to learn which skills matter and how pay grows. Headline findings: SQL appears in 90.5% of postings, and median pay jumps ~3.9x from fresher (₹4.8 LPA) to 5+ years (₹18.8 LPA).

Other work: an end-to-end COVID-19 insight engine (1M+ records, 150+ countries) and an IPL match analysis with interactive Plotly visualisations.
